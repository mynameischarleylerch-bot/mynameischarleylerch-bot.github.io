feat(angler): a full bag, end to end, through the real land path

Two tests that drive the game rather than the rule, which is what the unit tests
cannot do:

- Landing on a full bag keeps the CATCH and drops the FISH. The bestiary entry, the
  xp and the rank all still happen -- the fish was caught either way -- and the
  notice names the fish that did not fit, so a player watching a full bag sit there
  is never left guessing.
- An over-cap save is loaded whole. Deliberately NOT trimmed: silently deleting a
  dozen fish out of someone's save is far worse than letting them sell down. The cap
  governs what NEW fish may join, and the badge reports the truth as (14/10).

Mutation-tested: a notice that never fires, a notice that omits the fish name, a
full bag that silently keeps the fish, a call site that drops the cap, and load()
trimming to the cap were each caught.

One test bug worth recording. The notice assertions read "#notices .notice", but the
container is "#notify" (ui.notify = el('notify')). A wrong id reads as "no notice was
shown" -- the most misleading possible failure, because the notice really was on
screen. It was only caught by dumping the container innerHTML on failure, which is
worth doing whenever an assertion reports absence.

671 tests pass.
